Interest

Mechanics.

How machines turn energy into motion. I am drawn to the engineering behind engines, drivetrains, and vehicle electrical systems, where mechanical design meets electrical engineering.

Focus Areas

Where Mechanical Meets Electrical.

⚙️
Internal Combustion Engines

The thermodynamic cycle behind every engine: intake, compression, combustion, and exhaust. Efficiency, power output, and the limits that shape engine design.

🌀
Fuel & Air Management

From carburetion to electronic fuel injection: how air-fuel ratio is controlled, and why precise mixture control drives performance, efficiency, and emissions.

🔋
Vehicle Electrical Systems

Batteries, charging circuits, ignition, and wiring harnesses. The place where circuit theory from EEE meets a real vehicle.

🔩
Drivetrain & Transmission

Clutches, gearboxes, and final drive: how engine torque is converted into usable motion at the wheel.

🧭
Diagnostics & Reliability

A systematic approach to fault-finding: measure, isolate, verify. Preventive maintenance and reliability thinking applied to real machines.

⚡
Electric Mobility

Motors, battery packs, and power electronics in electric vehicles: where vehicle engineering is heading, and a natural fit for an EEE background.
